Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can Safey free up storage on my phone?
Yes. Move photos and videos into Safey's vault, then remove them from your Photos library and empty Recently Deleted. The memories stay safe and private in the vault while the space they used on your camera roll is freed. The free plan holds 500 MB; Pro (50 GB) and Max (200 GB) hold far more.
How do I hide photos and videos?
Open Safey's photo vault and import the pictures and videos you want to hide. They're moved into a Face ID-locked album and removed from your camera roll, so they never appear when someone scrolls your photos.
Is Safey really private?
Passwords, card numbers, CVCs, PINs and note contents are encrypted with AES-256 on your phone before they are uploaded. The key lives in your device's Keychain and never leaves it, so our servers only ever see ciphertext. Photos and videos are moved out of your camera roll into your vault and locked behind Face ID, but they are not yet encrypted at rest. We would rather tell you that than imply otherwise.
What can I store in Safey?
Passwords and logins, payment cards, secure notes, any files or documents (IDs, certificates, PDFs), and private photos and videos in a hidden, Face ID-locked album.
What happens if I lose my phone?
Your vault is locked behind Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, so no one can open Safey without it. Your data syncs privately, so you can restore everything on a new phone by signing in.
Is Safey free?
Safey is free to download and use with 500 MB. Safey Pro is optional: it starts with a 7-day free trial, then renews at the price shown in the app unless you cancel.
Is Safey available on Android?
Yes. Safey is free on Google Play for Android phones, with the same vault, decoy passcode and break-in alerts as on iPhone.
