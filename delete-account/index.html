Delete your Safey account

Deleting your account permanently removes it and everything stored in it — passwords, cards, notes, files, photos, videos and break-in photos — from every device and from our servers. It can't be undone.

In the app

iPhone: Settings → Delete Account. Then email us as below and we'll remove anything still stored on our servers for that account — the next iPhone update does this automatically.

Android: Settings → Delete account. You'll be asked for your password (or to confirm your Google sign-in) first.

Without the app

Email support@khatibdesigns.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done, within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what isn't

Your account and all the content in it are deleted. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that can no longer be linked to you may be kept.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Safey Pro subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play → Subscriptions (Android).
