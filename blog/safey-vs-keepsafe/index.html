Where they differ most

Scope. KeepSafe centers on hiding photos and videos. Safey does that too, but also stores passwords, cards, secure notes and files — one encrypted place for everything private, not just images.

Price. Safey's vault is free with no ads. KeepSafe is freemium, with some features behind Keepsafe Premium. If you'd rather not hit a paywall for core protection, that matters.

Encryption model. Safey encrypts on your device with AES-256 and keeps the key in your Keychain — the content is unreadable before it leaves your phone.

Which should you choose?

Choose KeepSafe if you specifically want a long-established, photo-only vault and use both iOS and Android. Choose Safey if you want a free, all-in-one iPhone vault — photos plus passwords and notes — with a decoy passcode and break-in alerts built in. FAQ

Is there a free alternative to KeepSafe?

Yes. Safey offers a full private vault — photos, videos, passwords, cards and notes — free, with no ads on the core app. KeepSafe uses a freemium model where some features sit behind a subscription.

What is the best KeepSafe alternative on iPhone?

For iPhone users who want more than a photo vault, Safey is a strong choice: it's free, uses AES-256 on-device encryption, and stores passwords and notes too, with a decoy passcode and break-in alerts.

Is my data encrypted in Safey?

Yes. Safey encrypts your data on your device with AES-256 (Apple's CryptoKit) and stores the key in the device Keychain, so content is protected before it ever leaves your phone.

Does Safey cost money?

The core vault is free. Optional paid plans add extra encrypted cloud storage (50 GB and 200 GB) if you want more room, but the app itself and its vault features are free to use.
