FAQ

What's a good alternative to Private Photo Vault?

Safey is a free iPhone vault that stores photos and videos plus passwords, cards and notes, all encrypted on-device with AES-256, with a decoy passcode and break-in alerts.

Is Safey free?

Yes — the core vault is free with no ads. Optional paid plans add extra encrypted cloud storage if you need more space.

Which is more private?

Both encrypt your content. Safey encrypts on your device with AES-256 and stores the key in the Keychain, and it never uploads decrypted content — so only you can read what's inside.

Can I store more than photos?

In Safey, yes. Beyond photos and videos it holds passwords, cards, secure notes and files — a single private place instead of separate apps.
